About us

Amani Yangu means “My Peace” — and that is what we hope you find here.

Established in February 2023, Amani Yangu offers emotional and psychosocial support to individuals, couples, and families — a safe space to breathe, reflect, reconnect, and grow.

Noxolo (Nox) Dlamini

Founder, Amani Yangu

Meet the founder

Walking alongside people toward healing and wholeness

Noxolo (Nox) Dlamini, the founder of Amani Yangu, has spent many years walking alongside people on their journeys toward healing and wholeness. Her work has taken many forms — inspirer, facilitator, pastor, and lay counsellor — all driven by a deep passion for supporting the wellbeing of others.

Her love for helping people led her to pursue formal training in psychology, where she earned her Honours degree. She later continued her studies with a Master’s degree in Health Promotion, which she completed cum laude. Her research focused on Mental Health, Psychosocial Support, and Peacebuilding — areas that continue to shape the heart of her work today.
