Meet the founder

Hi, I’m Nox.

Noxolo (Nox) Dlamini has spent many years walking alongside people on their journeys toward healing and wholeness — as an inspirer, facilitator, pastor, and lay counsellor.

She holds an Honours degree in Psychology and a Master’s in Health Promotion, completed cum laude, with research in Mental Health, Psychosocial Support, and Peacebuilding.
