01 · A good place to start

Coffee with Nox

Coffee with Nox is a gentle, individual-focused space for anyone who needs a listening ear — especially when you’re not quite sure what kind of support you’re looking for. These sessions are created for those moments when you feel stuck, overwhelmed, or unsure where to begin.

Sometimes you simply need someone to help you untangle your thoughts. Coffee with Nox offers exactly that: a calm, safe environment where you can speak freely while being guided with thoughtful, supportive questions. These conversations help you uncover clarity, identify solutions, and make sense of the things that feel confusing or heavy.

This space is especially helpful when you’re going in circles, seeking change, or struggling with challenges you can’t seem to resolve on your own. What begins as a simple, casual conversation can naturally grow into a series of meaningful coaching sessions — depending on what you need and what feels right for you.

02 · Healing & restoration

Counselling

At Amani Yangu, we offer both counselling and coaching — two supportive pathways designed to help you find healing, clarity, and renewed strength. Our counselling sessions create a safe, compassionate space for anyone carrying past hurts that continue to influence their life today.

Rooted in a faith-centred approach to wholeness, our work is gentle and grounded in genuine care. We understand that unburdening yourself can feel overwhelming, but having someone who listens deeply and walks with you makes the journey lighter. Our aim is to help you heal, grow, and rediscover peace in a meaningful and sustainable way.

What you can expect

Each session is tailored to your unique story. Together, we look at what’s weighing on your heart and explore ways to move forward with strength and clarity. You are welcomed exactly as you are — seen, heard, and supported.

Counselling can help you

  • Make sense of your brokenness
  • Release unresolved pain
  • Walk a personal journey of healing
  • Find peace despite past hurts
  • Build emotional and spiritual resilience
  • Learn to stand up for yourself with confidence
  • Develop and maintain healthy boundaries

04 · One-on-one

Individual Coaching

Individual Coaching at Amani Yangu is a personalised and empowering journey designed to help you grow, gain clarity, and step confidently into the life you desire. These one-on-one sessions offer a supportive, reflective space where you can explore your goals, identify what may be holding you back, and develop practical strategies to move forward with intention.

Our approach is gentle, thoughtful, and rooted in genuine care. Whether you are navigating a transition, seeking direction, or wanting to build healthier habits, individual coaching provides the structure, encouragement, and tools you need to thrive.

A journey designed for you

Each individual coaching journey is unique. Together, we explore your strengths, challenges, hopes, and dreams — and work toward real, lasting change. You are supported every step of the way, with compassion, clarity, and a sincere belief in your potential to flourish.

05 · For partners

Couple Coaching

Our Couple Coaching offers a supportive, non-judgmental space for partners who want to grow together. Whether you’re navigating communication challenges, feeling disconnected, facing recurring conflicts, or simply wanting to strengthen your bond, these sessions help you reconnect with clarity, compassion, and intention.

Our approach is gentle, structured, and deeply relational — creating an environment where both partners can feel seen, heard, and supported. Together, we work to improve communication, rebuild trust, and deepen emotional connection, helping you move forward as a more unified and resilient team.

Growing together, with intention

Couple Coaching is not about who is right or wrong — it is about understanding, healing, and building the kind of relationship you both desire. Each session is tailored to your unique dynamic, helping you reconnect, rebuild, and move forward together with greater confidence, harmony, and hope.

What to expect from couple coaching

  • A supportive, non-judgmental space where both partners can speak openly and feel heard
  • Guided conversations that strengthen communication and understanding
  • Tools to navigate challenges and work through recurring issues constructively
  • Strategies to deepen emotional connection and rebuild or strengthen your bond
  • Support in resolving conflict with confidence and compassion
  • A focus on shared goals and values, moving forward as a united, resilient team
  • A renewed sense of connection, where both partners feel seen and understood
  • A guided path toward a stronger, healthier partnership grounded in love and empathy
